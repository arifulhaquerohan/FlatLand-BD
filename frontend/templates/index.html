A few answers before you start

Visits, fees, listing checks and interior work. Find the details here, or ask our team directly.

Is FlatLand BD free to use?

Browsing, shortlisting and enquiring are free — always. Owners publish listings free too. If a cost ever applies to a service, it is stated before you commit, never added later.

How do I know the photos are real?

Every image is checked against the flat as it stands today before the listing goes live. Where our team visited, the photos were taken on that visit. If a listing cannot be verified, it does not publish.

Do you take brokerage?

Prices are published in full and any brokerage is stated separately, up front. There are no surprise charges at the doorstep — that commitment is the reason the review queue exists.

How fast will someone reply?

WhatsApp messages get a reply in about 15 minutes during working hours (Saturday–Thursday, 10:00–20:00), and 96% of enquiries are answered the same day.

I own a flat — how do I list it?

Post it in about two minutes from the listing form. It enters our review queue, the team checks identity, paperwork and photos, and it publishes once approved. You can delete it any time.

How do I arrange a flat visit?

Send the listing link and your preferred day to our team on WhatsApp, or use the contact form. We will discuss availability and confirm the visit details with you. You do not need an account to enquire.

Does verification replace my own document review?

No. Our checks help you choose which flats to explore. Before agreeing to buy, review the property documents and purchase terms independently, with professional help where needed. Ask the owner about any details you need clarified.

Can I request an interior design quote?

Yes. Browse the interior studios or send us your room sizes, budget and the work you have in mind through the contact form. A studio's starting price is a guide; ask for a quote covering your own project and scope.

Which areas do you cover?

Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet. Same-day site visits run across Dhaka, and listings in the other cities go through the same verification before they publish.
